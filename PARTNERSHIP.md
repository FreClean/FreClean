
FreClean × CeloHT

Strategic Partnership

Status: Active Strategic Partnership
Effective: 2026
Organizations: FreClean & CeloHT
Partnership Type: Strategic, Technology, Community & Impact Partnership

---

1. Overview

FreClean and CeloHT maintain a strategic partnership designed to connect sustainable cleaning solutions, entrepreneurship, digital financial inclusion, blockchain education, and community development.

The partnership brings together two complementary initiatives:

- FreClean — a cleaning products and services business focused on households, offices, Airbnb properties, and small entrepreneurs.
- CeloHT — a community-driven Web3 initiative focused on financial inclusion, blockchain education, decentralized services, and environmental impact.

Together, the organizations aim to create practical opportunities for communities to access products, services, education, digital payments, and entrepreneurship opportunities.

---

2. Purpose of the Partnership

The purpose of the FreClean × CeloHT partnership is to:

1. Promote practical entrepreneurship and small-business development.
2. Encourage responsible adoption of digital payments.
3. Support financial and Web3 education.
4. Create opportunities for local communities to participate in sustainable economic activity.
5. Support environmental initiatives and responsible business practices.
6. Explore the use of Celo ecosystem technologies in real-world commerce.
7. Develop initiatives that can be replicated in other communities and markets.

The partnership is focused on real-world utility and measurable impact, rather than speculative cryptocurrency activity.

or

only when the specific initiative genuinely involves CeloHT.

11. Financial Independence

Unless explicitly documented in a separate agreement:

- Each organization manages its own finances.
- Neither organization is automatically responsible for the debts or obligations of the other.
- Partnership activities do not create automatic financial obligations.
- Revenue generated by either organization remains under that organization's control unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Any joint funding, sponsorship, grant, investment, or revenue-sharing arrangement should be documented separately.
