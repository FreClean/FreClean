1. Introduction

FreClean is a professional cleaning services and sustainable products company dedicated to delivering reliable, high-quality solutions for residential, commercial, hospitality, and institutional clients.

FreClean operates with the ambition of becoming a recognized international brand in the cleaning and household solutions industry by combining:

- Professional service excellence
- Product innovation
- Entrepreneurship development
- Sustainable business practices
- Digital transformation

This Governance Framework defines the principles, structures, and standards that guide FreClean's operations, decision-making, financial management, partnerships, and long-term growth strategy.

---

2. Vision and Mission

Vision

To build a trusted global cleaning and lifestyle solutions company recognized for quality, innovation, sustainability, and positive economic impact.

Mission

FreClean provides professional cleaning services and innovative products while creating opportunities for entrepreneurs, employees, and communities through responsible business development.

Johnny Dubic is the Founder of FreClean and the creator of the company's original vision, mission, and strategic foundation.
