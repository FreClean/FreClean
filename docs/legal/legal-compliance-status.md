## Allowed status vocabulary

`VERIFIED`, `IN_PROCESS`, `PENDING`, `NOT_STARTED`, `NOT_AVAILABLE`,
`EXPIRED`, `RENEWAL_REQUIRED`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE` are the controlled status
values for this documentation set. A document's existence is not, by itself,
proof of compliance.
