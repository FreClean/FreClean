deadlines have been invented. Each entry should be assigned a verified due
date only after authoritative evidence or legal review is available.

## Reminder status vocabulary

Use only the following reminder statuses after a verified due date is known:

- `90 DAYS`
- `60 DAYS`
- `30 DAYS`
- `7 DAYS`
- `DUE`
- `OVERDUE`

Until then, the reminder status remains `PENDING`.
